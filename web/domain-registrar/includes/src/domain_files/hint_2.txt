Chocolate Cake Recipe

Ingredients: - 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour - 1 cup sugar - 1/2 cup
cocoa powder - 1 tsp baking powder - 1/2 tsp baking soda - 1/2 tsp
salt - 2 eggs - 1/2 cup milk - 1/2 cup vegetable oil - 1 tsp vanilla
extract - 1/2 cup hot water

Instructions: 1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). 2. Grease and flour a
cake pan. 3. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and
salt. 4. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Mix well. 5. Stir in hot
water until smooth. 6. Pour batter into pan. 7. Bake for 30–35 minutes.
8. Let cool before serving.

Enjoy your homemade chocolate cake!
